Our craft

Flour, water, salt — and a lot of patience

Every loaf starts two days before it reaches the counter. We keep a single levain alive since 2014, mill nothing we cannot trace, and bake in small batches so nothing sits overnight.

  • 48-hour cold ferment for deeper flavour and a lighter crumb.
  • Butter, eggs and milk from three farms within 60 miles.
  • Nothing artificial — if we cannot pronounce it, it stays out.
