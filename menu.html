Good to know

The questions the counter hears most.

How fresh is “baked this morning”?

Everything on the board is mixed and baked the same day it is sold. Anything unsold at close is donated — it never comes back out tomorrow.

Do you handle dietary requirements?

Yes. Vegan, nut-free and gluten-friendly options are marked on the board, and every celebration cake can be made with an allergy-aware recipe. Tell us in the order notes.

What notice do celebration cakes need?

Standard cakes need 48 hours. Tiered and wedding cakes need two weeks, including a tasting session at the bakery.

How do pickup windows work?

Slots run every 30 minutes from 7:00 AM to 8:30 PM. Orders are boxed at the start of your window and held on the warm shelf for one hour.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Up to 12 hours before your window, yes — no charge. Inside 12 hours we have already mixed the dough, so the order stands.

Allergen legend

Gluten, dairy, egg, nut and sesame are flagged on every product card. Full sheets live at the counter.

Vegan & gluten-friendly

Six standing vegan bakes and a rotating gluten-friendly loaf, baked on a separate bench.

Lead times

Pickup same day, cakes 48 hours, tiered cakes two weeks, corporate platters 24 hours.

Wholesale & offices

Weekly bread drops for cafés and offices, invoiced monthly. Minimum twelve loaves per delivery.

Need it for an event?

Catering, hampers and celebration cakes

Tell us the headcount and the date — we will build a platter, a tiered cake or a box of fifty éclairs around it.
